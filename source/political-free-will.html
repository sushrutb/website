Do You Have Political Free Will?

This quiz estimates how independently you form political judgments, and whether your views tend to track evidence, identity, or party loyalty.

Question 1 of 7
1. Are you able to list five achievements of a political party or government you do not support?
2. Are you able to list five issues where you disagree with a political party or government you do support?
3. Do you ever share or amplify positive actions taken by a political party or government you do not support?
4. When voting, what primarily influences your decision?
5. Have you ever changed your opinion on an important political issue?
6. When a new political issue emerges, do you quickly form an opinion that aligns with your existing political leaning?
7. Do you feel comfortable criticizing the political side you generally agree with?
