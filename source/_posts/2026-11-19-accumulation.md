The Beatles did not get a quota of how many number one hits they could create.

Taylor Swift does not need to limit how many people, or for how long, can listen to her music.

Picasso could make as many paintings as he liked.

There is no inherent limit to what you can create or accumulate. You are limited only by your skills and abilities.

**Business should be no different.**
