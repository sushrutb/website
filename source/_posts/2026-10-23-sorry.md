Being able to admit you have made a mistake and saying sorry is a superpower.

Most people will dilute their apology with excuses.

I missed our meeting because something important came up.

I said something because I was under stress.

I did not mean it that way.

Say you fucked up and will try not to fuck up in future.

That's it.
